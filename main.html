• The Romance: Set across two timelines, the story follows Kesu, a poor villager, and Sudha, a girl from a wealthy family, who fall deeply in love. • The Separation: When Sudha loses her gold bangle (kaakan), Kesu goes away to the city to replace or make a new one for her. Taking advantage of his absence, Sudha's family discovers their relationship and forces her to marry a rich man from another village. • The Aftermath: Heartbroken, Kesu withdraws from life and lives in isolation, while Sudha devotes her life to helping the needy. • The Reunion: 32 years later, a mischievous village boy named Gopi befriends the elderly Kesu and learns about his tragic past. Gopi sets out on a journey to find Sudha and reunite the star-crossed lovers, allowing Kesu to finally place the bangle on Sudha's hand before his final moments
